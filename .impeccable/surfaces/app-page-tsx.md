## Direction contract

THESIS: The rented house's own front door is the page. The category ships an engineer photo, badges and cards; this refuses it. The landlord never has to stand at this door, so the door does the talking.

OWN-WORLD: Refined, professional rendering of a London terrace front door. Limestone-white ground and white header, near-black ink, one committed oxblood field shaped as an arched door that holds the primary action, aged brass only on the postcode input and the house numeral. Thin encaustic tile rule (black, white, terracotta) as the only pattern, used as a divider. Heavy wide sans caps in enamel-nameplate style for brand and headings, plain workhorse sans for body. No drawn bricks, mouldings or illustration.

STORY: Visitor learns we arrange tenant access and do gas, electrics, alarms and EPC in one visit; believes they need not be there; posts their postcode through the letterbox to get a fixed price.

Right: full-height oxblood arched door from header to page bottom, gilt numeral at the crown, postcode label, brass-bordered input and white Get my fixed price button. A white example-property status card overlaps the door's lower left edge, with validity bars drawn to scale.
